## 1 · The goal

The Prospect Book grades agencies that are not yet WLIQ clients so the sales pod knows who to
chase first and how much each is worth pursuing: four reads (Fit, Qualification, Potential,
Signals) plus deal health, never summed; output is an anticipated tier, a chase-order key and
bands — never one composite score. It was built from the 9 Sep 2026 research document and its
four-phase build brief, iteratively, over about eight days, and the iteration caused drift.

**The foundation is not to be invented.** Prospect and account grading is a well-studied field
with settled science behind its mechanics. The book must rest on the industry's best-established
model — the platinum standard, drawn from the whole field's expertise — personalised and tuned to
WLIQ's needs, never reinvented. Every place the book diverges from that standard must be a
deliberate, recorded tuning to WLIQ, not an accident of iteration. (Owner's correction, 17 Sep.)

What the owner wants now:
1. To know where it actually stands — measured from the running system, not claimed by a document.
2. To know whether iteration broke the foundation, and to repair the breaks without tearing up
   what is right.
3. A structure that stops iteration doing this again, enforced by tests rather than memory.
4. To be able to trust the grades eventually — which means the calibration loop.
5. To hand the whole thing to another model or environment without losing anything.
6. To be able to trace every parameter in the book either to an established standard, with its
   source, or to a recorded WLIQ-specific ruling — and to know which.

End state: a book whose running system matches its record, whose grades can be validated, whose
next steps are ordered and owned, and whose every parameter is either the field's standard or a
deliberate, documented tuning of it.
